## 6. Why the service layer is separate from the controller

The controller only handles HTTP: it maps URLs, reads JSON, triggers bean validation and picks status codes. The service owns the business rules: USN normalisation and uniqueness, "PUT never creates", transactions and mutation logging. Keeping them apart means the same rules apply whether a request comes from REST, the startup seeder or a future batch job, and each layer can be tested on its own (the service with a mocked repository, the controller with a mocked service). It also lets the transport change, for example to GraphQL or messaging, without touching the logic that protects the data.
